# Waterline: video plan and narration (under 3 minutes)

About 280 words, so it fits in about 2:30 at a calm pace with room for pauses. Change any words that don't sound like you. Record in QuickTime (File → New Audio Recording), in a quiet room, phone face down. Leave a short pause at each line break.

Every beat has a small section label in the top-left corner and one caption line at the bottom. The Lens is on screen from the first second.

---

**[0:00–0:12] Label: "Danger, 150 cm". The water rising to the chin, then the "Dec 2021" line.**
This is where a hundred and fifty centimetres of floodwater reaches in a living room.
Up to my chin.

**[0:12–0:32] Label: "Bentong, Pahang". The room at Danger; captions carry the sources.**
I'm Shermaine, from Bentong, Pahang.
In December 2021, one resident in Kampung Kemansur said the water rose to one and a half metres inside her house.
A river reading means nothing in your own room.
So I made Waterline, to see the water before it comes.

**[0:32–1:35] Label: "One uncut run, Alert". The whole run at Alert, no cuts.**
On a dry day, I look down and pinch the things I'd save.
Then I choose a level. The names come from the Bentong river gauge.
Alert. Thirty centimetres.
The water rises to its real height, and the tag reads it off my body: above my ankles.
Only one thing glows: the lowest thing still under the water.
I grab it, carry it up, and let go near its pin. It snaps and turns green, and the next one lights up.
Everything is safe, so I switch off the mains.
The water drains, the rain turns into birds, and the Lens gets out of the way.

**[1:35–1:55] Label: "Danger, 150 cm". Crouch under the surface; the levels card.**
At Danger, five things are under water, and the plug is down at my feet.
If I bend, my eyes go under.
That's why there are three levels: move things at Alert.

**[1:55–2:25] Label: "How I built it". The brief, Claude Code with Lens Studio and the MCP connected, the LEAF panel, then before-and-after rooms.**
I planned it with the plan-lens skill. CLAD built it in Lens Studio through the Lens Studio MCP, and LEAF tests every done criterion.
Then I fixed it with design notes, over five rounds.
A full playthrough test found four moments with no cue. Now every step has one.

**[2:25–2:40] Label: "What's simulated". Then the end card.**
The room, my belongings and the levels are stand-ins. Lifting a real rice cooker needs real glasses.
Waterline. See the water before it comes.
